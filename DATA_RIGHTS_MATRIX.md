# DATA RIGHTS MATRIX

## Customer surfaces

| Surface | Default | Required proof |
|---|---|---|
| raw storage | DENY | agreement permits retention/storage |
| internal calculation | REVIEW | internal/non-display rights |
| customer display | DENY | distribution/display rights |
| API | DENY | machine-readable distribution rights |
| alert | DENY | notification/distribution rights |
| derived analytics | REVIEW | derived-work and distribution scope |
| historical replay | DENY | historical redistribution rights |

Successful calculation does not imply redistribution rights.

CME publishes separate licensing paths for internal display, internal non-display, distribution and customized/derived data products:
https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/license-data.html
https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/browse-data/derived-data.html

Premium news remains LICENSE_REQUIRED or LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED until an agreement covers storage, transformation and the customer surface.
