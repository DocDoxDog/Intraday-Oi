# Market Analyst V4 — Scenario-First Architecture\n\n## Why redesign\n\nThe current pipeline mixes structural levels, gamma context, and executable targets. V4 separates them so the analyst answers a market question rather than formatting numbers.\n\nPipeline:\nRAW SOURCES → CANONICAL STATE → MARKET MAP → SCENARIO ENGINE → RISK GATE → ANALYST NARRATIVE → TELEGRAM\n\n## One canonical Market Map\n\nEvery price level has exactly one role:\n- CONTEXT: Gamma Mean, Positive/Negative Gamma Zone\n- STRUCTURAL_RESISTANCE / STRUCTURAL_SUPPORT: source-derived levels\n- TRIGGER: event that activates a scenario\n- INVALIDATION: technical structure that disproves the thesis\n- EXECUTION_TARGET: structural level that also passes the RR gate\n\nGamma levels never silently become TP/SL. R1/R2/R3 and S1/S2/S3 are structural labels only.\n\n## Scenario state machine\n\nARMED → LEVEL_REACHED_WAIT_CONFIRMATION → CONFIRMED\n\nInvalidation can terminate a scenario at any stage.\n\nLONG requires: price acceptance beyond the structural trigger, HTF/M15/M5 alignment, M5 BOS, and acceptance/retest evidence when available.\n\nSHORT mirrors LONG.\n\nImportant: a snapshot showing price above a trigger does not prove that a breakout happened. Without crossing/acceptance history, the engine uses LEVEL_REACHED_WAIT_CONFIRMATION.\n\n## Invalidation\n\nUse the closest valid technical swing/structure on the opposite side of the trigger. The opposite option wall is only a fallback because using it as the normal stop can create artificial risk widths and destroy otherwise valid RR.\n\n## Structural path vs execution path\n\nExample: LONG trigger 4141, structural levels 4146 → 4161 → 4176.\n\nIf invalidation is 4130, then the structural path remains all three levels, but execution targets are only levels whose reward is at least 1R. Therefore 4146 can remain a map level without being called TP1.\n\nIf no level reaches 1R, the scenario remains valid but execution is blocked with NO_TARGET_GE_1R. That is different from saying the market has no bullish scenario.\n\n## Analyst responsibilities\n\nEvery run must answer:\n1. What regime are we in?\n2. Where is price relative to the map?\n3. What is the base scenario?\n4. What exact event activates it?\n5. What confirms it?\n6. What invalidates it?\n7. What is the structural path?\n8. Which levels are executable after RR?\n9. What evidence conflicts with the thesis?\n10. What is missing?\n\n## OI / GEX semantics\n\n- GEX describes gamma context; it does not prove dealer inventory direction.\n- OI change describes activity; it does not identify aggressor side.\n- Gamma zones are context, not automatic targets.\n\n## Telegram\n\nMessage 1: Market Read — regime, price location, flow/options, volatility, macro/news, conflict, thesis.\n\nMessage 2: Market Map — structural support/resistance, triggers, invalidations, gamma context, structural path.\n\nMessage 3: Scenario / Trade Plan — LONG and SHORT state, activation, confirmation, invalidation, executable targets, RR, and overall decision.\n\nTelegram must render the canonical object; it must not reconstruct or invent a trade plan.\n\n## State meanings\n\n| State | Meaning |\n| --- | --- |\n| ARMED | Trigger not reached |\n| LEVEL_REACHED_WAIT_CONFIRMATION | Price is beyond trigger but confirmation is incomplete |\n| CONFIRMED | Deterministic confirmation is complete |\n| INVALIDATED | Thesis condition has failed |\n| DATA_INSUFFICIENT | Required evidence is missing |\n\nNO_TRADE is reserved for an execution decision. It is not a synonym for an unconfirmed market scenario.\n\nExecution authority remains Ai-trader.
