# COMMERCIAL BENCHMARK

Status: RESEARCH BASELINE

## Product categories observed

SpotGamma:
options positioning/exposure analytics and market-level dashboards.

MenthorQ:
exposure/heatmap/matrix style market intelligence.

Unusual Whales:
fast options-flow discovery, filtering and alerts.

TradingView:
broad market terminal with configurable charts/watchlists and options-chain workflows.

CME QuikStrike:
institutional options analytics and market-data context.

## Common customer value

1. Fast answer to what changed.
2. Explainable market positioning.
3. Historical context.
4. Event/market linkage.
5. Alerts with low noise.
6. Drill-down evidence.
7. Saved views/workflows.

## Product differentiation hypothesis

AI Market Intelligence should differentiate on:

- canonical evidence chain
- OI/GEX/DEX/volatility in one state object
- news confirmation against actual market reaction
- conditional scenario plans rather than prediction claims
- source/version/data-age transparency
- Telegram-first delivery plus Vercel research terminal

## Anti-patterns

Do not copy competitor UI.
Do not make opaque proprietary scores the only explanation.
Do not optimize for maximum alert count.
Do not market AI as a replacement for the customer's broker or risk management.
