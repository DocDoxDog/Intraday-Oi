# NEWS SOURCE POLICY

Status: LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED

This policy separates source discovery from commercial redistribution rights.
No source is assumed licensed merely because its content is publicly viewable.

## Operating rule

Customer-facing output should prefer:
1. licensed source metadata
2. official public government data with explicit reuse terms
3. source links/attribution without reproducing protected full text

Do not store or redistribute full article text, images or feeds unless the contract explicitly permits it.

Source URLs and timestamps must be retained for provenance.

## Current source evidence

Reuters Connect states its subscriptions are licensed for commercial/editorial use with specific period/platform restrictions.
AP Media API exposes licensed multimedia content and states that availability depends on contract terms.
CME offers distribution licenses and derived-data licensing for products built from CME market data.
FRED terms require users of FRED API applications for others to link to FRED terms and note that individual series can be third-party owned.
EIA API terms permit using the API to develop services to search/display/analyze/retrieve EIA data, with attribution and non-endorsement conditions.
ECB states publicly released ESCB statistics may be reused free of charge when source is quoted and data/metadata are not modified, while third-party data are excluded.

These statements are source observations, not legal advice.
